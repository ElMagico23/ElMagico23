Hi, I'm Nico (Niccolò Dambrosio), an architect and computational designer, which is a fancy way of saying I argue with geometry and occasionally win. I earned an M.Arch at Politecnico di Bari and an MArch II at Harvard GSD, and I'm a PhD candidate at the Institute for Computational Design and Construction (ICD) in Stuttgart, where robots wind fibres into pavilions, which is exactly as fun as it sounds. I've worked on built things like the BUGA Fibre Pavilion and Maison Fibre, that is, buildings that stay up on purpose.

By day, I'm a Computational Designer in Footwear Innovation at adidas, where I make sure the interesting ideas survive contact with a production line. **[Mytilus for Grasshopper](https://github.com/MytilusGH/Mytilus)** is my after-hours plugin: a toolkit you can trust with your definition, and a few components you shouldn't leave alone with it. Made on my own time, on my own laptop, and on my own conscience. adidas was not consulted.

If something in it feels like magic, that's ElMagico's doing. If something breaks, I was away that day.
