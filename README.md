I'm Nico, a computational designer, architect by training. I was born in Bari, where the sea is warm and focaccia is in the air, and I now live in Portland, where the air is in the rain and focaccia is... MIA. Can't complain.

I studied architecture at the Politecnico di Bari, with a focus on archaeology (I still dig, only now in Rhino), and earned an MArch II at the Harvard GSD. I'm a PhD candidate at the Institute for Computational Design and Construction (ICD) in Stuttgart, where I teach robots to wind fibre into pavilions, and occasionally to open a beer. I've worked on built projects such as the BUGA Fibre Pavilion, Maison Fibre and the livMatS Pavilion.

By day I'm a Computational Designer in Footwear Innovation at adidas, designing lattices for projects so cool they are literally called Climacool. By night, ElMagico creates **[Mytilus for Grasshopper](https://github.com/MytilusGH/Mytilus)**: tools for geometry, meshes and the canvas. Not the ones your definitions deserve, but the ones they need. Plus a few that no one needs, but we all deserve. Made on his own time, on his own laptop. adidas was not consulted.
